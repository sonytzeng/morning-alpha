## Exact diagnosis (not an assertion that all three horizons shared one score)

1. `publicationReadiness.publicationDossier.evidence_ready` combines missing evidence, PIT issues **and licensing**. `member_eligible:false` and the validator's zero counts intentionally protected a review-only inventory. That inventory was incorrectly sufficient to report a research funnel: it has no complete/rejected partition and is not an evaluation engine.
2. `researchCard` marks every complete record WAIT_CONFIRMATION regardless of numeric research conditions. Missing families become insufficient, but no negative price/fundamental case can become an evaluated rejection. V1 is preserved as a completeness baseline, not a comparable strategy-performance baseline.
3. Existing `HORIZONS` are already different. **Not found:** SHORT requiring long financials, MEDIUM requiring supply chain, LONG requiring a volume breakout, or reuse of Production Recommendation thresholds. Do not invent those root causes.
4. Actual gaps: SHORT requires complete NEWS and INSTITUTIONAL despite a raw-activity question; foundation adapter emits only PRICE_VOLUME, not calculated TECHNICAL_STRUCTURE. MEDIUM requires ORDERS, GUIDANCE and INDUSTRY_EVENT with no maintained current producer. LONG's EPS alone cannot produce demand/moat/margin/capex/valuation. Gates are unsatisfiable **with the current producer set**, not universally impossible economic evidence.
5. Precise publication time is required for every V1 input. Official historical bars have valid as_of/first_seen/available_at but null publication time. This cannot support historical publication-time/reaction analysis; it can support a explicitly receipt-time observation after acquisition. Never synthesize the missing timestamp.
6. Foundation legacy TWSE cache has no additive `session_scope` label on earlier months. The new reader recognizes only the exact validated STOCK_DAY endpoint, matching stockNo/month/response; mismatches still fail. Original caches are not edited.
7. Existing V1 publication gate requires raw redistribution even for a conclusion-only output, and requires FORWARD_SHADOW. New use-specific **candidate** separates raw data, official facts and original analysis; no automatic blanket exemption for derivatives. Actual DB V1 admission/locked predictions remain unchanged.

## Rights by use (rechecked 2026-10-11)

The existing six precise OGL resources permit properly attributed facts/derivatives within that license; same-host historical endpoints do not inherit the grant. [OGL](https://data.gov.tw/license).
Our original wording does not automatically inherit copyright in raw data, nor automatically escape source contract obligations. Evidence-of-permission is separately modeled as storage, commercial, raw_redistribution, fact_publication, derived_publication, exact source/document, review time, expiry and attribution. Explicit derived permission can pass without raw permission; raw permission cannot imply derivative permission. Unverified input-derived analysis still fails closed.
[Fugle API terms](https://developer.fugle.tw/docs/data/intro/) reference exchange rules and third-party/derivative restrictions; the actual subscription contract is not present. [TWSE information use](https://www.twse.com.tw/zh/products/information/use.html), [TPEx purchased-data terms](https://eshop.tpex.org.tw/zh/product/shoppingTerm). These establish a need to verify scope, **not a conclusive ban on all independent analysis**. We did not accept a contract, purchase, contact a provider, or invent a grant.
Facts versus protected expression: do not publish company-site prose/graphics as OGL; a factual relationship must still have traceable evidence and reviewed commercial use. No source omission to launder dependent conclusions.
